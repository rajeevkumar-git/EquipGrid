1. Executive Summary & Problem Understanding

Institutions, laboratories, makerspaces, and colleges possess specialized equipment (3D printers, CNC routers, optical spectrometers, high-end workstations) that remain idle for significant intervals. Simultaneously, students and creators cannot access these resources due to exorbitant purchase or private rental costs.

EquipGrid provides an institutional resource-sharing ecosystem engineered around custody tracking, qualification checks, safety logging, and anti-monopoly fair allocation. Judges can experience the complete 10-step lifecycle without manual intervention via an integrated Judge Demo Switcher.

2. 10-Step Core User Lifecycle (Judging Verification Flow)

1. Resource Listed
2. Discovery & Rules
3. Booking Request
4. Eligibility Verified
5. Owner Review
6. Approved / Waitlisted
7. Check-in Condition
8. Return Condition
9. Damage/Dispute Log
10. Immutable Audit Trail
